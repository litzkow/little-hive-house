About us

A little house that works like a hive

Little Hive House is a husband-and-wife studio near Atlanta, Georgia, with Brazilian roots. We draw every design ourselves, then print, press and pack each magnet at home, one small batch at a time.

We started with the places we love and kept going: skylines, stars, flowers, bees and the little words that make a kitchen feel like home.

  • Made by handPrinted, pressed and packed by us.
  • Drawn in houseEvery design is our own artwork.
  • 2 × 2 inchesStrong magnet backs, glossy fronts.
  • Gift readyPacked in kraft boxes with our seal.
