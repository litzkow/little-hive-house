    Photo magnet questions

    What size are the magnets?

    Each magnet is a 2 × 2 inch square with rounded corners, a glossy front and a strong magnet back.

    Which photos work best?

    Use the original photo from your camera roll. Screenshots and photos saved from chat apps are often too small and print blurry; the builder warns you when a photo looks too small.

    Can every photo have a different frame?

    Yes. Tap any photo in the builder to pick its own frame and caption, or use "Apply this frame to all photos" to frame the whole pack the same way in one tap.

    Will you crop my photos?

    Yes, every photo is cropped to a square. Tell us in the notes if someone must stay in the frame and we will crop around them.

    How long does it take?

    Most packs ship within 3 to 5 business days. Shipping is $4.95 and free on US orders over $35.

    Can I mix my photos with your designs?

    Yes. Add photo packs and any designs from the shop to the same cart. Orders with 20 or more magnets get 10% off automatically, 50 or more get 20% off and 100 or more get 25% off.

    Need a custom design for an event?

    See our big order packages for weddings, parties, teams and businesses.
