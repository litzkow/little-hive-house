Questions

Good to know

How big is the magnet?

2 × 2 inches, a glossy photo-quality print on a strong flexible magnet, pressed by hand.

Can I put my own photo?

Yes. Designs with a photo let you upload yours, or pick one of ours. We check it before printing.

Will I see it before you print?

Yes. We email you a proof within one business day. Nothing is printed until you approve it.
